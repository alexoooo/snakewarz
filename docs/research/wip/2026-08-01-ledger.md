## P2 — strongest hand-written policy without a tree

### Brief

State: complete on 2026-08-01; findings are written into the agenda.

The candidate observes one live `BoardView`, scores legal destinations and returns. It may run
tail-aware flood/path/ownership reads, but never applies a transition, requests a playout, touches the
turn budget or allocates in `chooseMove`. Candidates remain internal and enter `:lab` through a
JVM-only research factory; they do not enter `ShippedBots`, acquire released ids or retain replays.

Three audits agreed that naive ownership and chamber readings are dead for this question:
`TempoOwnership.ownerOf(destination)` is always self and its distance is always one for the mover's
legal neighbours; global owned count, isolation, chamber worth and sealed share are constant across
the move set; and prior temperature cannot change an argmax. Those features are excluded. The one
ownership probe labels the mover's tempo-owned components after deleting the old head and attributes
the resulting component area to each destination. A chamber probe is deferred unless that cheaper
attribution fires and still leaves unexplained signal.

The predeclared ablations are one internal scorer configured as: guarded destination path; local
liberty/pinch/tail; local plus the 0.5 room guard; full guarded path plus local shape; full plus the
wall term as its own unresolved probe; and full plus owned-component area. Exact score ties are
reported before a pure position/destination hash chooses; no RNG or direction declaration order may
settle them.

Agreement uses at most one deterministic hash-min choice position per replay and progress third,
then the 1,000 lowest match-position hashes per map/third. It reports matches, forced turns, choice
positions, raw tie rate, unique top-1 agreement and expert-in-maxima ceiling. The teacher is the
strongest P1 fixed-budget point on that map: chamber alpha-beta on empty; territory alpha-beta on
arena and rooms; UCT@1000 on cross; PUCT@1000 on double spiral and islands. Islands also reports a
sensitivity reading against its statistically tied territory alpha-beta. Teacher cost is offline
label cost and does not enlarge the student's free lane.

Cost runs next in Chrome on the same six P1 boards, five paired passes and zero consumed evaluations;
the 0.25 ms free-lane maximum is a hard gate. Only then do strength fields run. The five general
boards use 200 mirrored rounds with fresh seeds 22,001/32,001/42,001/52,001/62,001, stock `chase`,
every surviving ablation and `uct@600`; no replays retain temporary ids. The one-third-gap point
thresholds are -210 arena, -176 cross, -239 rooms, -310 double spiral and -283 islands, derived from
P1's chase-to-UCT@600 ratings. A qualifier clears three point thresholds and its interval is not
wholly below Chase on either other board. A forfeit voids a field.

The first Chrome policy-cost pass was void: all 36 cells failed the declared four-of-five paired
control gate. Chase is only a few microseconds here, while Chrome's observed monotonic-clock tick is
100 microseconds, so single-call control means were quantisation noise rather than evidence of
machine drift. Before any rerun, the harness was fixed to retain one raw subject call per sampled
position for the 0.25 ms worst-turn gate while timing 100 additional calls as one batch for the mean
used by paired-control stability. The policy is deterministic and stateless between calls apart from
reused buffers; every call still sees the same live turn and consumes zero evaluations. All cells are
rerun because none survived the first pass.

Two batched passes exposed a second instrument defect before classification. Their printed integer

side of seven microseconds could truncate to six and seven and fail. Those classifications are void
too. The final harness prints the same integer summary but compares full batch totals and decision
counts by integer cross-multiplication, with no division or floating point. One final whole-target
run supplies every cost cell; earlier raw maxima are not cherry-picked into it.

The final pass put every candidate inside the free lane, and the five strength fields qualified the
full-owned rule. It ships as `cartographer` in a map-specialist section, outside the empty-12 ladder.
The complete bots and app Chrome targets and the repository-wide build passed after release-surface
and portrait-count consistency fixes.

2026-08-01. The source is bound to the shipped rules, empty 8x8, the exact P5 champion spec and the

canonicalisation or browser table is attempted in this feasibility run.
