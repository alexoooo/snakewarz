SnakeWarz Snake Warz

Tron-style snakes as an AI testbed

Last snake moving wins. Walls and every trail — including your own — are lethal, and there is nothing to eat.

Controls

Click a square
One step towards it, around anything in the way.
Hold
Keeps going along that route until you let go.
Drag
Draws the exact route: it follows the pointer square by square, and stops where something is in the way.
Arrow keys or WASD
Steer one square at a time. Hold a key to keep going that way.
The arrows beside the board
Steer one square at a time. Hold one to keep going that way.

Gauntlet

Ten opponents and a Final Boss, and as many attempts as you like. Beat one and the next unlocks.

Tournament


        

            The bots in the slots under Setup, on that board and from that seed. Head-to-head
            plays every pair that many times, each seed from both seats — rows beat
            columns. Free-for-all seats all of them in every match — rows outlasted
            columns.
        

    






    

        
Replay

        
    

    

        
        
        
        

            Watch replay winds a finished match back and plays it again on the board behind this
            panel — Start match returns to live play. The link carries the whole match, move
            by move, no server involved; open one and it plays back here too.

Speed

            How fast a match between bots plays. A match with you in it moves when you press a key
            instead, so this changes nothing until you are out of it.
        


        
        
Keys

        

            Arrow keys or WASD to move — with you in the match nothing happens until you press
            one, and holding a key keeps going that way. Space plays and pauses a match
            between bots and . steps it one turn; on the board those two belong to the
            match, so Enter is what presses a button there.
        

        

            Tab moves between the controls and Enter presses the one you are
            on — including Play again, so a lost match costs one key. Esc closes
            whatever is in front of you, and closes the board back to the menu.
